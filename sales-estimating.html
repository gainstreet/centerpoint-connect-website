Sales & Estimating

Track leads. Close more deals.

Everything you need to turn a lead into a signed proposal — in one system built for commercial roofing.

Step 1 · Capture the Lead

Track every prospect from day one

Referrals, walk-ins, web forms, or phone calls — Centerpoint keeps every lead organized from the start. Log contact info, assign reps, and monitor deal status from a centralized CRM that works in the office or the field.

  • Lead and opportunity tracking
  • Assign by rep, location, or service — 1 to 100 locations
  • Task reminders and automated follow-ups
  • Centralized client timeline with notes, files, and past jobs
  • Mobile CRM access for field reps

2
Step 2 · Qualify the Job

Get the right details before you quote

Send a rep to the site, log inspection details, mark problem areas, and upload photos — all from the mobile app. Inspection data stays tied to the client profile and flows straight into the estimate.

  • Structured inspection reporting
  • Annotated photo uploads
  • Site-specific notes stored with the lead
  • PDF inspection exports for internal or client use
Product screenshot
3
Step 3 · Build the Estimate

Create accurate proposals in minutes

Using a built-in commercial roofing price book, your team builds photo-rich proposals fast — no spreadsheets, no duplicated effort. Drop in scope items, photos, disclaimers, and branded formatting in a few clicks.

  • Preloaded commercial price book
  • Drag-and-drop proposal builder
  • Add photos, exclusions, and payment terms
  • Branded PDF generation
  • Mobile-ready estimating from job sites
Product screenshot
4
Step 4 · Send & Track

Deliver the proposal and see what happens next

Send the estimate through Centerpoint and get notified the moment your client opens it. Schedule follow-ups or trigger reminders automatically — clear visibility into where every deal stands.

  • One-click proposal delivery
  • Email open and view tracking
  • Automated follow-up scheduling
  • Convert to job with one click
Product screenshot
5
Step 5 · Track & Optimize

Measure what works. Improve what doesn't.

Clear dashboards show rep performance, deal flow, conversion rates, and projected revenue — so you can double down on what's working.

  • Sales dashboards by rep, division, or service type
  • Conversion tracking and lead-source analytics
  • Weighted revenue forecasting
  • Pipeline visibility by deal stage
Product screenshot
6
Step 6 · Handoff to Production

From signed proposal to active job — instantly

Once a proposal is approved, turn it into a live project in one step. Scope, pricing, photos, and notes pass straight to production — no handoff errors, no duplicate work.

  • One-click job conversion
  • All scope and files carried into production
  • Syncs with scheduling, dispatch, and materials
  • Less admin time, better communication
