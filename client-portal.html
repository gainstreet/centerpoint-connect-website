Client Portal

Manage roofs and expectations.

Give clients real-time access to their jobs, documents, budgets, and communication — without extra admin from your team.

Step 1 · Invite the Client

Set up access in just a few clicks

Once a job or ticket is underway, grant your client access to a personalized portal. Choose what they see — budgets, documents, schedules, or photos — and let them log in from anywhere.

  • Portal access by job, property, or account
  • Permission-based visibility
  • Invite links emailed to the client contact
  • Secure login and branded interface

Step 2 · Share Real-Time Info

Clients see what's happening — without calling you

The portal shows what matters: current job status, ticket updates, uploaded documents, and pending approvals. Clients see progress as it happens — no more status calls clogging your phone lines.

  • Live job and ticket status view
  • Milestone updates and scheduling info
  • Change-order visibility
  • Budget and approval tracking (when enabled)

Step 3 · Share Files, Photos & Reports

Deliver documentation with a professional touch

Upload inspection photos, close-out reports, permits, and warranties straight to the portal. Clients download what they need — no hunting through emails or asking your office to resend.

  • Photo albums and galleries
  • PDF reports and file folders
  • Document version history
  • Branded download links

Step 4 · Manage Approvals

Get faster approvals and fewer delays

Need a green light on a change order, repair quote, or job phase? Clients review and approve inside the portal — keeping things moving without back-and-forth calls or emails.

  • Optional approval workflows for proposals and changes
  • E-signatures or one-click confirmations
  • Notifications when client action is needed
  • Timestamped approvals for record-keeping

Step 5 · Transparent Communication

Showcase your professionalism through clarity

Clients leave comments, ask questions, and respond to updates in one place. No mixed messages, no lost texts — everything stays on record and tied to the project.

  • Message thread attached to each job or ticket
  • Comment history per item or update
  • Office and field staff visibility
  • Client notifications for new messages

Step 6 · Strengthen Relationships

Build confidence with every click

Informed clients are confident clients — and that builds loyalty. The portal makes your company look organized, responsive, and in control, without adding work for your team.

  • Consistent, professional client experience
  • Fewer status calls and admin tasks
  • Self-serve access to historical projects and files
  • All communications and documents archived automatically
